# Datasets

* [d3fend LinkML schema](d3fend-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML model of MITRE D3FEND 1.6.0, `src/d3fend/schema/d3fend.yaml` (schema id `https://w3id.org/lmodel/d3fend`): an umbrella over a core and an entries module, whose tree root `D3fendCatalog` holds every term of the edition as a record.
* [Core module](core-module.md) - `src/d3fend/schema/d3fend_core.yaml`, the module with the schema's two value types, `d3fend_date_time` and `d3fend_uri`, and the one list, `D3fendOwlKind`.
* [Entries module](entries-module.md) - `src/d3fend/schema/d3fend_entries.yaml`, which `scripts/d3fend_to_linkml.py` writes from the Turtle: the abstract `D3fendEntry`, its 22 kinds, and one slot for each of the ontology's 292 properties a record can hold, under the property's own IRI.
* [The catalogue](catalogue.md) - `src/d3fend/data/d3fend-1.6.0.yaml`: every class and individual of D3FEND 1.6.0, 5,419 in all, as one record each with its IRI as `id`, its kind as `type`, and its text and relations as slots, without the 340 abstracts of cited sources.
* [Generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - What `just gen-project` and `just gen-doc` generate from the schema: the projections in `project/`, the Python models in `src/d3fend/datamodel/`, and the merged schema and pages under `docs/`. None is edited by hand, and the SQL generator is off.
* [Example data](example-data.md) - The examples `tests/test_data.py` checks: a valid example of each class, most of them real records of D3FEND 1.6.0, a counter-example for each constraint, and a counter-example for each rule on a whole catalogue.
* [Copy of the upstream](upstream-copy.md) - `upstream/`: D3FEND 1.6.0's Turtle file, the licence file at tag 1.6.0, and the Terms of Use, unchanged. `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` pins each by its git object hash, and a test holds the folder to that list.
* [The schema's adapt copy](adapt-copy.md) - `.lokf/d3fend.yaml`, the copy `just lokf-adapt` writes from the schema so that it sits beside LOKF's `lokf.yaml`: rooted on `D3fendEntry` and `D3fendCatalog`, with `id`, `type` and `rdf_type` dropped for LOKF's, and six keys renamed with a `d3fend_` prefix.
* [Neighbours' adapt copies](neighbour-copies.md) - The adapt copies of fourteen released lmodel repositories in `tests/neighbours/`, among them attack, cwe, capec and nist-sp-800-53, each pinned by its SHA-256, against which `tests/test_schema_rules.py` holds this schema's copy apart and `tests/test_mappings.py` holds the mappings.
* [File Analysis](example-defensive-technique.md) - The defensive technique `d3f:FileAnalysis` of D3FEND 1.6.0, written as a concept of type `D3fendDefensiveTechnique` in the adapt copy's keys. It shows that the domain schema admits a technique as a concept, with its relations to D3FEND's own terms.
